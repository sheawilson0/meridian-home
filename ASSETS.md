# Included third-party assets

Four fonts under `public/design/type/fonts/` are distributed with their unmodified `OFL.txt`: Doto, Meridian Dot and Meridian Blob. They use the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The fork's public copyright attribution is retained as required. Source: https://github.com/oliverlalan/Doto and the Meridian font fork. These are the only allowlisted files inside the research directory.

Ten planet textures under `public/img/planets/` are from Solar System Scope / INOVE, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/. Venus uses the atmosphere texture. The release manifest pins each exact byte sequence and records the corresponding source URL. The images were reviewed; research screenshots and personal images are excluded.

The optional tester's rrweb 2.1.7 recorder/player files retain `tester/public/vendor/rrweb/LICENSE` (MIT, rrweb contributors). Source: https://github.com/rrweb-io/rrweb.

UI SVG marks are included as source. PNG app icons, fallback art, header photos, personal portraits, screenshots, native downloads and audio are excluded pending separate approval. Remote Google Fonts retain their own family licences and fallback fonts; no remote font files are copied by this export.

The application licence and branding rights still need the owner's decision before release.
